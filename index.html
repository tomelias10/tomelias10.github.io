ORYNVAL

Research on the memory cost of long-context inference.

At long context, decode is bound by how many bytes of KV cache each step reads from HBM, and by how much of that cache fits on a GPU. That is what sets GPU count. ORYNVAL is an early-stage research company working on reductions in GPU inference cost that we can defend mathematically, targeting KV-cache memory and bandwidth in long-context serving.

Status: research stage. No validated results yet.

What we are working on

  • KV-cache memory footprint and HBM bandwidth per decode step at long context.
  • Compression and selection schemes for the KV cache, evaluated against published baselines under equal memory budgets.
  • Measurement that holds up in real serving engines (vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM), not only in isolated kernels.

We publish what fails as well as what works. We will not quote performance numbers until they reproduce.

Pilot early access

For teams running substantial inference workloads. We will contact a small number of teams when there is something worth evaluating on real traffic. Signing up does not commit you to anything.

Research updates

An occasional technical note when we have something new: an analysis, a measurement, or a negative result. No marketing.

What we publish

Short technical analysis of published inference research: what a result does and does not show, the formula behind it, and where it breaks. Example: why cross-layer KV sharing buys capacity, not decode speed.
